## The problem

Organisations run on decisions, but the way those decisions are made is usually invisible — threads, calls, documents, no single source of truth.

## The approach

DDecide turns proposal → discussion → verdict into one auditable flow. Every option is stated, every argument is threaded, and every vote records its reasoning. Access controls mirror the org, so the right people weigh in at the right stage.

## The outcome

A clean, accountable path from idea to decision — with the trail of how you got there always one click away.

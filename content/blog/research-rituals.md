# The research rituals that keep a product team honest

Teams drift when opinions outrun evidence. Small research rituals keep shipping grounded without slowing the sprint to a crawl.

## Rituals worth keeping

1. **Talk to one user every week** — not a survey dump, a real conversation.
2. **Write the decision memo** — what you heard, what you chose, what you will revisit.
3. **Re-test the happy path** after every meaningful release.

These habits are cheap. Ignoring them is expensive.

## Engineering angle

Backend and frontend both benefit: clearer requirements, fewer speculative features, and APIs shaped around real workflows instead of imagined ones.
